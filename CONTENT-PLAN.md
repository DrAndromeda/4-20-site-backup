# Content plan — wave 1 (20 pages per language)

Slugs identical across languages; text written natively per language (see CONTENT-GUIDE.md).

## Batch 1
1. `about` pillar ≥1500 — what cannabis is; botany (Cannabis sativa L., hemp vs drug-type, legal hemp THC thresholds vary); plant parts; chemistry overview; brief history; subspecies debate (indica/sativa/ruderalis vs chemovars); ComparisonTable.
2. `cannabinoids` pillar ≥1500 — endocannabinoid system (CB1/CB2, anandamide, 2-AG); phytocannabinoids; acid precursors and decarboxylation; overview table THC/CBD/CBN/CBG/CBC/CBDV/THCV; entourage effect and its evidence limits; approved cannabinoid drugs (Epidiolex, nabiximols where approved, dronabinol/nabilone) stated accurately; safety.
3. `terpenes` pillar ≥1500 — what terpenes are; plant role; volatility/boiling points; TerpeneGrid (myrcene, limonene, alpha/beta-pinene, beta-caryophyllene, linalool, terpinolene, bisabolol); honest evidence (mostly preclinical; caryophyllene–CB2 documented); indica/myrcene "couch-lock" critique; storage losses.
4. `cannabinoids/thc` spoke ≥800 — chemistry (C21H30O2, 314.46, ChemicalDiagram), CB1, dose-dependent effects, onset/duration by route, tolerance, risks (anxiety, cognition, cannabis use disorder, adolescent brain, psychosis risk, driving), THCA, delta-8 note.
5. `cannabinoids/cbd` spoke ≥800 — chemistry (C21H30O2, 314.46), non-intoxicating, mechanisms (not a direct CB1 agonist), approved use (Epidiolex), limited evidence for anxiety/pain, CYP450 interactions and liver enzyme elevations, product-quality/labelling problems.

## Batch 2
6. `growing` pillar ≥1500 — cultivation as botany/horticulture information: photoperiod vs autoflower, indoor/outdoor, lifecycle overview, environment by stage (temperature, humidity, 18/6 veg, 12/12 flower, pH soil 6.0–7.0 / hydro 5.5–6.5), GrowCalendar (northern hemisphere outdoor), common problems, prominent legal note (cultivation illegal/restricted in many places).
7. `consumption` pillar ≥1500 — harm-reduction overview of routes (smoking, vaping, edibles/oils, sublingual tinctures, topicals), onset/duration ranges (avoid false precision), risks per route, ComparisonTable, who should avoid (adolescents, pregnancy/breastfeeding, psychosis history, cardiovascular), driving, interactions. No encouragement.
8. `law` pillar ≥1500 — global regulation: UN conventions (1961, 1971, 1988), models (prohibition, decriminalisation, medical, regulated adult-use), hemp thresholds, examples (Netherlands, Canada 2018, Uruguay 2013, Germany 2024, USA federal vs state, Thailand 2022, Spain, Ukraine, Russia), laws change → official sources; ComparisonTable of models; no evasion advice.
9. `growing/seeds-and-germination` spoke ≥800 — seed biology, viability/storage, germination methods, ~22–25°C, timelines, failures, legal note; `howTo` frontmatter 4–6 steps.
10. `growing/lifecycle` spoke ≥800 — stages germination→seedling→vegetative→pre-flower→flowering→harvest, durations, sex expression (dioecious, hermaphroditism under stress), light-cycle role, ComparisonTable of stages, trichome maturity basics.

## Batch 3
11. `terpenes/myrcene` spoke ≥800 — most abundant terpene in many cultivars; hops/mango/lemongrass; bp ~167°C; evidence preclinical; "mango before smoking" folklore lacks solid evidence.
12. `terpenes/limonene` spoke ≥800 — citrus aroma; citrus peel oils; bp ~176°C; limited, mostly animal/in-vitro evidence.
13. `consumption/dosing-and-safety` spoke ≥800 — "start low, go slow" with commonly cited harm-reduction ranges (attributed carefully), delayed edible onset, overconsumption symptoms and when to call emergency services, storage away from children/pets, alcohol mixing, driving, tolerance. medical: true.
14. `law/ukraine` spoke ≥800 — controlled substance; administrative vs criminal liability by official thresholds (describe cautiously, they change); medical-cannabis reform adopted by the Verkhovna Rada in late 2024 with implementing rules to follow (hedge; point to zakon.rada.gov.ua and the Ministry of Health). Not legal advice.
15. `faq` spoke — 10–12 real questions in frontmatter (addiction, detection time, hemp vs marijuana, CBD vs THC, vaping safer?, memory, overdose, legality, driving, pregnancy, medical vs recreational), answers 30–80 words; body ≥300 words introducing and pointing to detail pages.
16. `legal` legal ≥300 — educational purpose, no medical/legal advice, 18+ audience, no sale of products, accuracy/update policy, external-links disclaimer, only essential localStorage keys ('age-ok','cookie-ok','lang').
17. `sources` legal ≥300 — editorial and sourcing policy, evidence hierarchy (systematic reviews > RCTs > observational > preclinical), corrections; list of reference organisations with links (NIDA, NCCIH, EUDA, WHO, FDA, PubChem, PubMed).
18. `medical` hub 400–700 — honest overview: approved drugs; stronger evidence (chemotherapy nausea, MS spasticity, certain refractory epilepsies) vs weaker (pain, anxiety, insomnia); physician supervision. medical: true.
19. `strains` hub 400–700 — what a strain is; unreliable names; chemovar/chemotype; certificates of analysis; limits of indica/sativa labels.
20. `substances` hub 400–700 — harm-reduction framing of other substances discussed alongside cannabis (alcohol, nicotine, diphenhydramine/"DPH" misuse); why mixing is dangerous; no misuse dosing; DPH recreational use is dangerous (delirium, cardiac toxicity at high doses).
