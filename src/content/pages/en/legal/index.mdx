These terms explain what this website is, what it is not, and how you can use it. By using the site you accept them. If you disagree with any part, please stop using the site.

## Educational purpose

**This site is an educational reference about cannabis, its chemistry, effects, risks and legal context.** It is written to inform, not to encourage use. Nothing on it should be read as a recommendation to consume any substance, and the site does not promote cannabis or related products.

## No medical advice

**The information on this site is general and is not medical advice.** It cannot diagnose, treat or prevent any condition, and it is not a substitute for a consultation with a qualified healthcare professional. Do not delay or avoid seeking medical help because of something you read here. Pages that make health-related statements carry a disclaimer and describe the strength of the evidence as honestly as possible. In an emergency, contact your local emergency services; in Ukraine, call 112.

## No legal advice

**Descriptions of laws are general summaries, not legal advice.** Drug laws differ between countries, change over time and are applied according to individual circumstances. For your own situation, consult a qualified lawyer and the official texts, such as those published on the Verkhovna Rada portal for Ukrainian legislation. See also [law in Ukraine](/en/law/ukraine/).

## Audience: adults only

**The site is intended for people aged 18 and over.** An age confirmation is shown on your first visit. If you are under 18, please leave the site. Parents and guardians who want to understand the topic can read the pages on [dosing and safety](/en/consumption/dosing-and-safety/) and the [FAQ](/en/faq/).

## No sale of products

**This website does not sell cannabis or any related product, and it does not provide information on where to buy or how to obtain such products.** It does not host advertising for them, and it does not facilitate transactions of any kind.

## Accuracy and updates

**We aim for accurate, current information, but errors and outdated statements are possible.** Pages show the date they were last updated. Science and law both change, and we revise pages when new evidence or regulation appears. Corrections are handled as described on the [sources](/en/sources/) page. We make no guarantee that content is complete, and you use it at your own risk to the extent permitted by law.

## External links

**Links to third-party websites are provided for reference.** We do not control those sites, are not responsible for their content or privacy practices, and a link does not mean endorsement. External pages can change or disappear.

## Cookies and browser storage

**The site uses only essential browser storage and no advertising or tracking cookies.** It saves three items in your browser's localStorage:

- `age-ok`, which remembers that you confirmed you are an adult;
- `cookie-ok`, which remembers that you dismissed the storage notice;
- `lang`, which remembers your preferred language.

These items stay on your device, are not used to identify you and can be removed at any time by clearing your browser's site data. If you clear them, the age notice, storage notice and language choice will simply appear again.
