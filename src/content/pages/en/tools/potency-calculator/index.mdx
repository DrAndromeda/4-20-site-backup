Enter weight, THC percentage and, if listed, THCA percentage. The tool multiplies grams by 1000 and by the percentage, adds THCA scaled by 0.877, and divides by the number of servings. Use it to understand how concentrated a product is, not to decide a dose. If you are unsure, speak to a healthcare professional.
